Frequently Asked Questions About Our Coatings & Supply

How do you guarantee an exact color match for vehicles in Qatar?

We utilize digital spectrophotometers that analyze pigment variation and metallic flake alignment under multiple light spectra, calibrated against global OEM formula databases.

Can you prepare custom aerosol spray cans for specific car paint codes?

Yes, we offer same-day custom aerosol filling. Bring your vehicle's OEM color code or a fuel flap sample, and we formulate ready-to-spray pressurized 1K/2K aerosols.

Do you supply paint booth protection and maintenance products?

Yes, we supply waterborne peelable booth coatings that trap overspray and keep cabin walls bright white, along with ceiling and floor filter media.

What brands do you officially distribute in Qatar?

We are authorized distributors for HB Body, Challenger, Axalta, Rupes, Vilote, and DCRYSTA coatings and bodyshop equipment.

Do you deliver directly to workshops across Doha?

Yes, our logistics team supplies workshops, collision repair centers, and industrial facilities throughout the Industrial Area and across greater Doha.

What products are recommended for marine and yacht coatings in Qatar?

We supply specialized 2K polyurethane topcoats and high-build epoxy barrier coats specifically formulated to resist intense Gulf UV radiation and high salinity.
